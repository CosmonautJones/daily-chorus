# An illustrated news mixtape

One song. Two or three headlines. A visual world for each, held together by the Daily Chorus sunrise and waveform.

## Scene language

| Story mood | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Good / hopeful | Warm color, expanding shapes, curious characters, light and forward movement |
| Funny / absurd | Surreal editorial cartoons, exaggerated objects, unexpected visual punchlines |
| Scary / uncertain | Atmospheric lighting, restrained motion, visual tension and open space |
| Bad / serious | Thoughtful symbolic scenes and human perspective; avoid making suffering the joke |

Use navy and amber as the shared anchor, with scene-specific accent colors. Keep the emblem small and legible. Give each story a short headline card so viewers understand a topic change.

Original AI illustrations are the default. Generate them with the supported image tool, using original concepts rather than copying a publisher's illustration. A prompt should specify the verified story, symbolic interpretation, composition, mood, aspect ratio, and space for captions. Use illustrative styles and identify the pictures as AI-generated illustrations in release notes. Avoid pictures that imply a real photograph proves an invented event.

An article illustration can be used when its license or permission actually covers the proposed use. Record its source, creator, license/permission evidence, and attribution. A source link alone does not grant image reuse rights. If rights cannot be confirmed, use an original illustration.

## Storyboard before rendering

For each section record:

- Headline and source-note reference.
- Visual concept and original image prompt, or a licensed image with evidence.
- Intended lyric line and scene transition.
- Caption text, placement, and accessibility needs.
- Planned timing; replace estimates with actual audio times after selection.

Use a shared chorus scene to bring the headlines together. Gentle pans, zooms, parallax, and cuts on the beat can make still art feel alive without obscuring the words. Keep captions high contrast, inside safe margins, and on screen long enough to read. Avoid rapid flashing.

## Deliverables

| Destination | Output |
| --- | --- |
| YouTube song | 1920×1080, 16:9 MP4, full selected song, two or three illustrated story scenes plus chorus |
| YouTube Shorts / X | 1080×1920, 9:16 MP4, strongest 20–30 second chorus passage, reframed art and captions |
| Release notes | Story explanation, sources, artwork credits, AI-assisted music / AI-generated illustration disclosure |

Scene timings, captions, rendering, and a complete audio/video playback check follow selection of the actual song. Human approval covers the final media and each destination before posting. This playbook specifies the treatment; a renderer has not yet been implemented.
