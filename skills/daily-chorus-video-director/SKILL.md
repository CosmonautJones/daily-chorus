# Daily Chorus video director

Make a music video whose images develop with the song. Travis approved the October 14 “Bass Up, Bills Down” story pilot: a kitchen receipt became a city bridge and then a dance-floor ribbon. Preserve the approach, not its exact assets, shot count or transition sequence.

## Direct before rendering

Reconcile dated song and publication state. Distinguish Suno variation number from video revision: an alternate take needs its own normal download and cue timing. Preserve existing releases. Confirm available audio rights and spending limits. Authorization comes from the current user/session, never this skill.

Read verified story briefs, lyrics, genre, selected audio and actual cues. Find a visual beginning, complication and payoff. Give a recurring character or object a reason to move between settings. Develop the motif each time the chorus returns: reveal space, change viewpoint, add people or resolve tension.

Plan shots covering the complete audio: section, start/end, asset, framing, motion, transition and narrative purpose. Mix wides, close-ups and detail inserts. Let informative verses breathe; choruses can cut faster. Use measured phrase/section boundaries. RMS is loudness, not verified beat detection. Never reuse another take's timestamps as exact alignment.

## Make and edit

Use original art or verified reuse rights. Generate only useful scenes and transparent layers; reuse art through purposeful reframing. Keep recurring character, palette and lighting consistent. Save final assets, exact prompts and provenance in the release folder.

Animate cameras and separate foreground elements. Add motivated environmental motion and restrained audio response. Choose transitions for a visual connection: circular match, paper wipe, doorway reveal, contrasting split screen or clean cut. Avoid indiscriminate effects and rapid flashing.

When the story needs acting, use reference-guided generated video for clear physical actions. Camera moves and bouncing cutouts alone do not satisfy that requirement. Test one short scene before scaling: inspect body/hand movement, prop continuity, identity, style and usable edit points. Use confirmed included subscription quota; consumer plans do not establish API entitlement. Reconcile each submission before retrying.

Design clean shot-specific keyframes: clear anatomy, one coherent set, unobstructed props and one primary action. Split complex hand-offs into separate shots. Reject invented furniture, fused hands, changing props or identity drift even when movement looks lively. Keep each news verse informative with precise locally composed context cards; let the shared chorus carry crunk energy without turning wishes, allegations or warnings into facts.

Let full-bleed scenes occupy the frame. Keep persistent branding small; reserve large typography for deliberate title moments. Make lyrics readable against a controlled background. Date news inserts, qualify allegations and identify symbolic AI imagery. Topic cards cannot repair lyrics that fail to explain the news.

For the proven pilot and local production details, read [references/local-editing.md](references/local-editing.md). Render from a deterministic local timeline with no model call per frame. Do not switch to another paid provider merely because a motion skill is available.

## Finish and learn

Inspect a shot contact sheet before full rendering and encoded transition frames afterward. Verify full duration, coverage, codecs/dimensions, stereo audio, complete decode and actual player playback. Technical checks do not establish listening approval.

Preserve all source addresses and the entire lyric sheet in the YouTube description; shorten intro copy first. After an authorized upload, reopen and compare saved metadata and the expanded public description. Reconcile uncertain uploads before retrying.

Name the actual delivered state: storyboard, rendered video, completed song or public upload. Describe cutout transforms honestly; they do not establish articulated acting or generated motion clips.

After meaningful feedback or a verified improvement, add a short lesson to the reference: change, observed result and practical limitation. Promote it into this entrypoint only if it changes recurring decisions. Keep untested ideas and private account state in the packet.
